Gestures

  • Tap a cell to write. Drag across steps to set the length.
  • Drag a note to move it. Drag its right edge to resize.
  • Pinch to zoom. Slide two fingers to scroll.
  • Hold a cell or note for the radial menu.
  • A to P play. Z and X move the octave. 1 to 5 hit drums. Space plays.
  • C4 and C5 down the side start each octave. C4 is middle C.
  • Pen pressure sets the volume of a new note.
