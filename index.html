What the controls do

Gestures

  • Tap a cell to write. Drag across steps to set the length.
  • Double-tap a note to remove it. One tap leaves it.
  • Erase removes the note you are on. It does not stay on.
  • Drag a note to move it. Drag its right edge to resize.
  • Pinch to zoom. Slide two fingers to scroll.
  • Hold a cell or note for the radial menu.
  • Hold a button, a knob, or a slider name for a tip. A pencil hover shows it too.
  • A to P play. Z and X move the octave. 1 to 5 hit drums. Space plays.
  • Pen pressure sets how loud a new note is, and the bar on the note shows it. A finger or mouse uses VOL.
